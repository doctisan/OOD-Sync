## 1. What OOD Sync is

OOD Sync is an internal automation operated by OOD Studio. It synchronizes design briefs from a client agency's project boards to the studio's task board and copies the reference files those briefs link to from one Google Drive account to another. It is used only by the operator and the studio's designer. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

## 2. Who may use it

Only accounts expressly authorized by the operator may connect to the application. By completing Google's consent screen for one of those accounts, you confirm that you own or are authorized to use that account and the files it contains.

## 3. Permitted use

The application may be used only to:

- read folders and files linked in a design brief on the agency account;
- copy those files to the studio account under the folder `01-OOD`;
- post links to the copied files on the studio's task board.

Any other use, including accessing files that are not linked in a brief, modifying the agency account or redistributing the copied files outside the studio, is outside the scope of the application.

## 4. Your responsibilities
